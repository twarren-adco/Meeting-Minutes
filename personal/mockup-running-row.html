Running row: more options

Variations on option 4: Weinam tasks that run over several days are listed once, above or beside your My minutes day columns, and appear in a day column only on the day they're due. Example week: today Tuesday 3 Nov, with your current TW tasks. I've added one made-up overdue task, marked example, to show how overdue work looks. None of your Weinam tasks are overdue right now.

What happens to an overdue task?

It stays in the running area until it's marked Complete (or N/A) in Weinam. It moves to the top, turns red and says how late it is, e.g. "Overdue 5 days · was due 29 Oct". It doesn't appear in a day column, because its due day has passed. It carries into next week's view too, so it can't drop off unnoticed.

4A · Grouped: overdue, due this week, runs on

The running row split into three short lists.
  • Overdue first in red, then tasks due this week, then tasks that carry on past Friday.
  • Each group is a plain checklist, the same style as your minutes.

4B · Compact table

The running row as a small table with a days-left column.
  • One row per task: ref, task, status, started, due, and days left (or days overdue).
  • Sorted by days left, so the most urgent is always first. Overdue rows are tinted red.

4C · Progress lines

Each running task with a thin bar showing how far through its time it is.
  • The bar fills from start date to due date, so a nearly full bar means it's almost due. A countdown ("13 days left") sits on the right.
  • Overdue tasks show a full red bar and "5 days over".

4D · Side column

Running tasks in a column on the left instead of a row on top.
  • The left column lists running tasks with countdowns and progress lines, and stays beside your five days.
  • Keeps the day columns starting at the top of the panel, so your minutes are visible straight away.

4E · Summary chips you can expand

A one-line summary that opens into the full list.
  • The row shows one chip per task with its days left, coloured by status. Overdue and In action chips stand out.
