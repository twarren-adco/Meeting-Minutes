Weinam tasks in My minutes: four options

The week looks like your screenshot: five day columns of your minutes, nothing else. The difference is where Weinam tasks that run over several days appear. Example: this week, today Tuesday 3 Nov, with your current Weinam tasks. 3.1 and 3.4 run 2–16 Nov; 3.5 runs 3–20 Nov; 3.6 runs 2–6 Nov; 1.14 and 5.2 started last week and are due Wednesday. Weinam lines carry a small blue ref tag; orange boxes are In action. Duplicates are already removed.

Option 1 · Today while running, then the due day

Running tasks sit in today's column, like an unticked line that rolls forward.
  • While a Weinam task is running it appears in today's column only, with "day 2 of 15 · due 16 Nov" underneath.
  • Future columns show a Weinam task only on the day it's due.
  • Past days show only what you wrote that day.

+ Today shows everything that's live; the rest of the week stays clean.− Today's column gets longer when several tasks are running.

Option 2 · Start day and due day

A Weinam task appears twice: when it starts and when it's due.
  • On its start day it shows "Starts · due 16 Nov". On its due day it shows "Due".
  • Tasks that started before this week show on Monday as "Running from 29 Oct".
  • Nothing in between, so the middle of the week only has your own lines.

+ Marks the two dates that matter; very little repetition.− Mid-week you can't see what's still running unless you look back to Monday.

Option 3 · Pinned box at the top of each day

A small "Running" box heads every column, your minutes below it.
  • Each day opens with a compact box listing the Weinam tasks running that day (ref and short name, orange if In action).
  • On the due day the task moves out of the box into the list as a proper line with "Due".
  • Your own minutes stay underneath, exactly as now.

+ Every day shows what's running, but kept apart from your to-dos.− The same box repeats across all five days.

Option 4 · One "Running this week" row above the days

Running tasks listed once, as checklist lines, across the top of the week.
  • A single row above the five columns lists the Weinam tasks running this week, in the same checklist style, with their due date.
  • A task due this week also appears as a line on its due day.
  • The day columns are just your minutes, as in your screenshot.

+ Each running task shows once; no repetition in the columns.− They sit apart from the day you'll actually work on them.
